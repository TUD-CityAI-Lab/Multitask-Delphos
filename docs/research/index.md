# Research & Papers

Delphos formulates discrete choice model specification as a sequential decision-making problem. An agent constructs utility specifications through a sequence of actions and learns from rewards returned by an Apollo estimation environment.

The research documentation covers:

- the Markov Decision Process formulation;
- state, action, reward, and task representations;
- single-task and multitask reinforcement learning;
- training, fine-tuning, inference, and transfer; and
- the experiments and repositories associated with each paper.

See [Papers and Reproducibility](papers.md) for publication links and the corresponding code repositories.
