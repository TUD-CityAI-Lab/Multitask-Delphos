# Papers and Reproducibility

## Paper 1: Single-task Delphos

**Delphos: A reinforcement learning framework for assisting discrete choice model specification** introduces the sequential decision-making formulation and the original single-task agent.

- [Paper record and manuscript](https://ssrn.com/abstract=6430865)
- [Reference implementation and experiments](https://github.com/gnova3/delphos-single-task)
- [Canonical datasets](https://github.com/TUD-CityAI-Lab/transport-choice-datasets)

An immutable code release and final journal citation will be added when the archival publication is available.

## Paper 2: Multitask Delphos

The second paper extends Delphos to multitask reinforcement learning, fine-tuning, and transfer across heterogeneous choice datasets.

- [Training, fine-tuning, and experiments](https://github.com/gnova3/Delphos-training)
- [End-user inference package](https://github.com/gnova3/Delphos)
- [Canonical datasets](https://github.com/TUD-CityAI-Lab/transport-choice-datasets)

The public paper link, immutable reproduction release, and final citation will be added when the manuscript is released.
